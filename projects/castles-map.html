Castles of Navarre

An interactive 3D map of 80 castles and fortifications in Navarre, with a timeline from the 8th century. I built it from open data: Wikidata, OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia, joined and cleaned in Python.

Why

I saw LAYRS: Amsterdam, a map of every building in Amsterdam coloured by the year it was built, and wanted to try the same idea with Navarre's castles. Most of them no longer stand, so the question was how many there were, when they appeared and what is left of them.

How it works

  1. Collect. SPARQL queries to Wikidata for castles, fortresses, towers and gates in Navarre, plus an OpenStreetMap export of everything tagged as a castle or fort.
  2. Match and clean. Python joins both sources by Wikidata ID, then by distance and name. It merges duplicates, translates Basque-only names and sets aside 211 records that aren't castles: 139 prehistoric hillforts and archaeological sites, 27 city gates and walls, and 45 manor houses and other buildings.
  3. Enrich. One record per castle, written from its Wikipedia articles: date, current condition, a short history and the source, with a confidence level on every record.
  4. Map. MapLibre GL with open elevation tiles for the 3D relief, OSM rivers and main roads, a timeline and a record panel for each castle. No map server: everything ships with the page.

Stack

Python, pandas, SPARQL (Wikidata), Overpass API (OpenStreetMap), Wikipedia API, MapLibre GL JS, GeoJSON, terrain tiles.

What the data shows

  • The oldest documented castle in the kingdom is Leguín, near Urroz. Abd al-Rahman III's army destroyed it in 924.
  • 41 of the 61 dated castles first appear between 1000 and 1300, when the kingdom held its frontiers with Castile, Aragon and al-Andalus.
  • Many of the vanished castles were pulled down on purpose. After the conquest of 1512, Castile ordered Navarre's fortresses demolished.

Data quality

Every record says how far its source backs it. 55 are confirmed, 3 are doubtful because the sources disagree, 9 are partial and 13 have no source yet. Several of those 13 only appear in a catalogue that blocks automated access, so they will be checked by hand.
